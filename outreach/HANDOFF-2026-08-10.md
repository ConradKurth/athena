## How to draft a new batch (pipeline is deterministic + gated — reuse it)
1. Write `.context/drafts/batch_<name>.json` = `{id: template_key}` and
   `.context/drafts/out_<name>.json` = `[{id, email, greet, opener, hook}]`.
   Templates: `A_creator`, `B_caregiver`, `P_podcast`, `E_publication`, `C_community`.
   Supply `email` (these tail rows have no `contact_email` in the CRM) and `greet`
   (`"there"` for org/team inboxes, first name or `Dr. X` for a person).
2. Render: `R1B_PREFIX=<name> R1B_BATCH=.context/drafts/batch_<name>.json
   R1B_OUT=.context/drafts/out_<name>.json python3 outreach/tools/render_round1b_emails.py`
   — must print all 3 gates clean (gate 2 dash-scan fails closed).
3. Stage: `export GMAIL_SA_KEY=/Users/conrad/workspace/cyclone/athena/gcs-service.json;
   export GMAIL_IMPERSONATE=conrad@athenauraguard.com;
   MANIFEST=outreach/drafts/<name>-send-manifest.csv LOG=outreach/drafts/<name>-gmail-draftlog.csv
   SLEEP_SECS=1 bash outreach/tools/push_gmail_drafts.sh`
