# Contact-form + DM copy — compliant / claim-free (2026-08-09)

Short variants of the awareness pitch for channels with length limits and no
"mail you a free set" framing. **Claims: dryness / overnight / thin / inside-the-brief
/ physician-founded / dignity only.** No antibacterial / UTI / bacteria / 96% / infection /
prevention. No em/en/double dashes. `{opener}` = one personalized sentence on their work.

---

**Reframe rules vs the email templates**
- Forms/DMs open a conversation, they don't ship product blind: ask permission before mailing.
- Keep under the field limit; drop the second product sentence if space is tight.
- DM: no links in first message (platforms suppress reach / flag spam); offer to send details on reply.
- Always personalize `{opener}` on their actual work. A missing `{opener}` is a hard abort.
